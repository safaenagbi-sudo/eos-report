\chapter{Supplementary Outputs}
\label{app:supplementary_outputs}

This appendix is reserved for supplementary project outputs that support
the results and discussion chapters, such as additional maps, extended
diagnostic figures, exported artefact summaries, or supplementary result
tables. The main diagnostic figures and core quantitative results are
reported directly in Chapters~\ref{ch:results} and~\ref{ch:discussion},
while the detailed methodology and modelling references are provided in
Appendices~\ref{app:detailed_methodology_tables} and
\ref{app:model_evaluation_details}.
